# Y-SYNC

**Your YouTube, premium-style — free and open source.**

Y-SYNC is a lightweight YouTube client for Android. Watch videos and
**live streams while they are on air** (not just replays), with the
features that are normally locked behind a paywall:

- **No ads**
- **Background playback** — keep listening with the screen off
- **Popup / picture-in-picture** player
- **Downloads** — save videos and audio to your device
- **Live streams** — watch broadcasts live, with rewind up to 4 hours
- Resolutions up to **4K**, subtitles, playlists, local subscriptions and history
- No Google account needed — subscriptions and history stay on your device

Also supports SoundCloud, media.ccc.de, PeerTube and Bandcamp.
